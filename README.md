About us

BORTECH SYSTEMS (we, us) is open-source project/community centered around logic engineering and developing automated systems in PL5's BRM5 RGE.

It started when PL5 introduced triggers and buttons into RGE which revolutionized automation, now almost anything can be done without any ZUES-ing. The aim/goal of BORTECH SYSTEMS is to share knowledge, document techniques, and help grow the engineering side of the BRM5 community and therefore make better and more interactive (or less static) maps for operations for factions or groups, and also just to make cool shit.

	The project is build around a few core ideas:
	
	- Open-source development: documentation, code, models and research is shared openly so others can learn from, improve and build upon them.
		
	- Engineering education: from simple tween-ing blocks & models to complex mortar platforms, we aim to teach how to create stuff and how triggers & buttons and stuff works in a understandable way.
	
	- Community collaboration: developers, engineers, builders, testers and experimenters can contribute ideas, improvements, give feedback etc. to improve existing systems or even develop something entirely new.
	
	- Research and experimentation: The project encourages innovation in RGE, whether its optimizing model part count or code, breackthroughs are needed to continue creating new and better systems for everyone to enjoy.




Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision of BORTECH SYSTEMS is to become a collaborative engineering hub for the BRM5 community. A place where practical systems, ideas, research, and knowledge is shared freely.

All systems, documentation, and development resources are distributed publicly through the BORTECH SYSTEMS Discord and GitHub.




	Terminology
	
	"ZEUS-ing" refers to manual intervention by operation administrators or ZEUS controllers during BRM5 operations, such as: spawning bots, moving vehicles or props, controlling environmental events & manually simulating systems.
	BORTECH SYSTEMS focuses on reducing dependency on ZEUS intervention through automation and modular logic systems.
	
	"tween-ing" refers to a command in RGE called "tween" that is used to move models smoothly from its current position to another position
